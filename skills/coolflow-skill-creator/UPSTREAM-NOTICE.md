# Upstream research and notice

This Skill was independently written for CoolFlow after studying [Superpowers `writing-skills`](https://github.com/obra/superpowers/blob/2ce8aa15d05bd57213e6f746f953aafb7ded7a8b/skills/writing-skills/SKILL.md), repository commit `2ce8aa15d05bd57213e6f746f953aafb7ded7a8b` (reviewed 2026-11-02). The research included its [discovery guidance](https://github.com/obra/superpowers/blob/2ce8aa15d05bd57213e6f746f953aafb7ded7a8b/skills/writing-skills/references/discovery.md), [testing guidance](https://github.com/obra/superpowers/blob/2ce8aa15d05bd57213e6f746f953aafb7ded7a8b/skills/writing-skills/references/testing-skills-with-subagents.md) and [repository license](https://github.com/obra/superpowers/blob/2ce8aa15d05bd57213e6f746f953aafb7ded7a8b/LICENSE).

We adapted the ideas of baseline comparison, scenario-based validation and concise discovery metadata. Unlike the upstream workflow, this Skill does not require the Superpowers TDD Skill or a particular subagent runtime, does not demand deletion of existing work, and keeps unverified drafts distinct from accepted assets. It adds CoolFlow-specific source assessment, overlap checks, offline baseline handling and human promotion. This notice is not a license declaration for the CoolFlow repository.
